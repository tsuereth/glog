I wish that <game:U.V.S. Nirmana>'s store page was clearer about what "kind" of game it is -- especially what it isn't. **Nirmana is *not* a story told with puzzles**, like <game:Kaizen: A Factory Story> or <game:Exapunks> or even <game:SpaceChem>. It's more like <game:Molek-Syntez> or the sub-games within <game:Last Call BBS>: puzzles, with a theme.

The puzzle mechanics *aren't bad*, though they are definitely non-straightforward. And I'd say the lack of narrative justification, an absence of context for its core conceit, makes Nirmana feel more confusing than it has to be.

That core conceit being ~~digital~~ metaphysical signal processing. ... I think.

![]($SiteBaseURL$uvs-nirmana_nagarahara.gif){width=925 height=550}

Even setting aside its sense of mechanical strangeness, though, I'm simply not interested in solving the puzzles for their own sake. A single line of flavor text preceding each challenge is not enough to keep me engaged.

Not to downplay the effort it takes to *invent* puzzle mechanics, and implement challenges around them. But "just puzzles" isn't sufficient motivation for me.
